# GHM V127

- Ask GHM: only composer/context dock remains fixed at bottom of workspace; header, modes and conversation scroll together.
- Cook from Home built-in recipe library can expand/collapse.
- Recipe shopping actions now require confirmation before adding items.
- Pantry seasonings (salt, cumin, turmeric, spices, oils, etc.) are treated as groceries for shortage/full recipe shopping. Household water remains manual-only.
- New UI text is localized for English, Gujarati, Hindi and French.
